# Next.js

Recipes for apps built on the Next.js App Router. Anything that needs your API key runs in a route handler.

- [Stream text to speech in Next.js](./01-stream-speech.mdx): stream Sonic 3.6 audio through a route handler and play it as it arrives.
- [Turn an article into a podcast in Next.js](./02-article-to-podcast.mdx): fetch an article, write a two-host script with an LLM, voice it with Sonic 3.6 and play it with a synced transcript.
- [Transcribe and summarize meetings in Next.js](./20-transcribe-and-summarize-meetings.mdx): record in the browser, transcribe with ink-whisper and write notes and action items with an LLM.
- [Narrate a story with a different voice per character and highlight each word](./30-multi-voice-narration-with-word-timestamps.mdx): one Sonic context per line, word timestamps for read-along, and Ink 2 to hear the listener's choice.
- [Build a real-time two-way voice translator with Ink 2 and Sonic](./40-real-time-voice-translation.mdx): Ink 2 turn events start the translation, an LLM streams it, and Sonic 3.6 speaks it in the other language.
